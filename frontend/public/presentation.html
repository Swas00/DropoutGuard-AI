The Problem

The $4.2 Billion Higher Education Attrition Crisis

Traditional institutional detection is retrospective, siloed, and too late to prevent dropouts.

32.9%
Global Dropout Rate
Nearly 1 in 3 undergraduate students never finish their degree, disrupting career mobility and lifelong earnings.
Post-Mortem
Retrospective Detection
Universities flag academic failure only after semester grades are posted—when intervention is already too late.
Siloed Data
Fragmented Signals
Attendance, LMS activity, fees compliance, and exam grades sit in disconnected databases with no unified risk intelligence.
The Institutional Blind Spot:

When a student begins slipping in week 4, faculty members have no automated trigger. By the time final backlogs accumulate, the student has already disengaged mentally and financially.

The Solution

Proactive, Explainable, & Prescriptive Intelligence

A multi-stakeholder platform connecting University Deans, Faculty Advisors, and Students.

Prescriptive Action

Closing the Loop: Faculty Intervention Workflow

Transforms analytical predictions into immediate, accountable human interventions.

✉️
Automated Nudge Dispatch

Dispatches non-punitive, supportive SMS, email, and WhatsApp notifications reminding students of upcoming assignments and attendance recovery.

👨‍🏫
1-Click Mentor Assignment

Routes the case directly to the student's designated faculty advisor (e.g. Prof. Ananya Sen) with complete academic telemetry and history.

📋
Audit & Progress Tracking

Tracks intervention status across four stages: Identified ➔ Assigned ➔ In Progress ➔ Successfully Resolved. Full FERPA logging.

Institutional Accountability:

Every logged counseling session is recorded with mentor notes, scheduled dates, and follow-up metrics—ensuring no student slips through institutional cracks.
